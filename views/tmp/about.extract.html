Our Journey

From Hiralal Mannalal & Sons to Surendra Pulses across four generations.

1965

The Foundation

Founded by Mr. Hiralal Goyal as Hiralal Mannalal & Sons, establishing our roots in ethical trading and local grain sourcing.

1973

Industrial Expansion

Factory established by Mannalal Goyal under Surendra Pulses, bringing modern processing technology to our operations.

1988

Product Diversification

Mr. Surendra Goyal joined the business and played a key role in expanding operations, doubling turnover and diversifying product range.

2015

Modern Era

Harsh Goyal joined as the 4th generation, driving digital transformation and aggressive global export growth.
